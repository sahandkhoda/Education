# 👨🏻‍🎓 Bachelor of Science in Medical Engineering

This folder contains work completed during my **Bachelor of Science in Medical Engineering (180 ECTS)** at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**.

Medical Engineering is an interdisciplinary field where engineering, natural sciences, and medicine come together to develop technologies that improve healthcare. The programme provides a broad foundation in mathematics, engineering, programming, and medical sciences, preparing students to solve technical challenges within modern healthcare through analytical thinking, practical engineering, and scientific problem-solving.

## 🧭 Programme Structure

The programme combines fundamental engineering education with medical applications, gradually building both technical competence and domain knowledge over three years.

Students begin with core courses in mathematics, physics, programming, and engineering sciences before progressing to more specialized subjects in medical engineering. Throughout the programme, theoretical studies are complemented by laboratory work, programming assignments, design projects, and scientific reports, providing practical experience alongside academic knowledge.

## 🧠 Programme Highlights

Throughout the programme, students develop knowledge in areas such as:

- 📐 Mathematics
- ⚛️ Physics
- 💻 Programming
- ⚡ Electronics
- 🎛️ Control Engineering
- 🩺 Medical Technology
- 🧬 Physiology & Anatomy
- 📊 Statistics
- 🧠 Biomedical Signal Processing
- 🤝 Engineering Design & Innovation

The programme concludes with a **Bachelor's Thesis**, where students apply the engineering principles, technical skills, and scientific methods developed throughout the degree to an independent research or engineering project.
