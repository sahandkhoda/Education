# 🔬 Bachelor's Thesis

This folder contains material related to my **Bachelor's Thesis** in Medical Engineering at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**.

The Bachelor's Thesis marks the culmination of the programme, providing an opportunity to apply the engineering principles, technical skills, and scientific methodology developed throughout the degree to an independent research or engineering project.

Depending on the project, this folder may include:

- 📄 Thesis report
- 💻 Source code
- 📊 Data analysis and results
- 📈 Figures and visualizations
- 📁 Supporting documentation

The thesis demonstrates the ability to independently investigate a technical problem, evaluate potential solutions, and communicate the results in a scientific and professional manner.
