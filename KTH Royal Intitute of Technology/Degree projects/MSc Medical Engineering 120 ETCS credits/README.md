# 🧙🏻‍♂️ Master of Science in Medical Engineering

This folder contains work completed during my **Master of Science in Medical Engineering (120 ECTS)** at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**.

Medical Engineering is an interdisciplinary field where engineering, computer science, physics, and medicine come together to develop technologies that improve healthcare. The programme combines a strong theoretical foundation with practical engineering experience, preparing students to design, evaluate, and advance medical technologies used in both clinical practice and biomedical research.

## 🧭 Programme Structure

One of the strengths of the programme is its flexible structure. Students first choose an engineering **track**, which builds expertise in a core technical discipline:

* 💻 Computer Science
* ⚡ Electronics
* ⚛️ Physics

The chosen track is then combined with a medical engineering **specialization**, allowing students to apply their technical knowledge within a specific healthcare domain:

* 🦴 Biomechanics
* 🏥 Health Systems
* 🖼️ Medical Imaging

This combination enables students to tailor their education to their interests while developing both deep technical expertise and domain-specific knowledge.

## 🧠 Programme Highlights

Throughout the programme, students develop advanced knowledge in areas such as:

* 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
* 🧠 Biomedical Signal Processing
* 📊 Statistics & Data Analysis
* 🧪 Simulation and Modeling
* 🔬 Scientific Methodology
* 🩺 Medical Technology
* 💻 Software Development
* 📈 Research Methods
* ⚖️ Ethical Considerations

The programme concludes with a **Master's Thesis**, where students apply advanced engineering methods to an independent research or development project within medical engineering.
