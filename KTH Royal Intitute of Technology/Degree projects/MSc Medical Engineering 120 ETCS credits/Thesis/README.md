# 🔬 Master's Thesis

This folder contains material related to my **Master's Thesis** in Medical Engineering at **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**.

The Master's Thesis is the final component of the programme and represents an independent research or engineering project within the field of medical engineering. It builds upon the advanced knowledge and specialization acquired throughout the programme, allowing students to address complex challenges through scientific investigation and engineering design.

Depending on the project, this folder may include:

- 📄 Thesis report
- 💻 Source code
- 📊 Data analysis and results
- 📈 Figures and visualizations
- 📁 Supporting documentation

The thesis demonstrates the ability to independently plan, conduct, and present advanced engineering work while applying scientific methods to solve real-world problems.
